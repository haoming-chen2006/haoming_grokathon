# New Starter Handbook

Everything someone needs in their first two weeks, in one document they can read
end to end in under an hour.

```project
name: New Starter Handbook
category: documents
budget: 10
areas:
  - Week one: accounts, access, who to ask, what to read
  - How we work: meetings, decisions, where things are written down
  - Benefits: the parts people actually ask about, in plain language
```

## What good looks like

A new starter gets to the end and has no unanswered question that would have
stopped them doing their job on day three.
