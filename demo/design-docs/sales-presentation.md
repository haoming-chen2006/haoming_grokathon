# Aeris Chairs — Q3 Sales Push

We are pitching the Aeris ergonomic chair line to mid-market office fit-out buyers.
The deck goes to a 30-minute first meeting, so it has to land the category story in
five slides and leave twenty minutes for questions.

```project
name: Aeris Chairs — Q3 Sales Push
category: slides
budget: 25
areas:
  - Research: what buyers in this segment already believe, and what our last three wins had in common
  - Narrative: the five-slide arc, from problem to proof to ask
  - Visuals: product photography, one comparison chart, one customer logo wall
  - Voiceover: a 60-second version for people who cannot take the meeting
```

## What good looks like

A buyer who has seen four chair decks this quarter should be able to say what is
different about ours without being told twice.

## Constraints

- No claim about back-pain outcomes without a citation we can show.
- Customer logos only where we hold written permission.
- The 60-second cut has to work with the sound off.
